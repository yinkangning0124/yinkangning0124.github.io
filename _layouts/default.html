Kangning Yin 殷康宁

Humanoid robot learning at Shanghai Jiao Tong University

I’m a Ph.D. student at the Apex Lab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Professor Weinan Zhang. My research focuses on robot learning, especially for humanoid robots.

Before that, I received my Master’s degree from ShanghaiTech University, advised by Professor Zheng Tian. I was a research intern at the Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute, working with Professor Jianyu Chen on agile humanoid locomotion, and at TeleAI, where I worked with Dr. Chenjia Bai on whole-body controllers for humanoids. I received my Bachelor’s degree from the Harbin Institute of Technology.

Email Google Scholar GitHub

Research

I work on humanoid robot control, motion tracking and navigation, along with character animation, 3D human motion generation and motion retrieval. My long-term goal is to bring humanoid robots into everyday life.
